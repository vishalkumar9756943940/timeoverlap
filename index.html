Frequently Asked Questions

How does TimeOverlap handle Daylight Saving Time (DST)?

Calculations are evaluated against the specific date selected using the browser's native IANA timezone database. Transitions in the US, UK, and Europe are automatically calculated.

Is team data saved on any server?

No. All timeline intersections run client-side in your browser. Shareable links encode the configuration strictly in the URL hash/query string.
